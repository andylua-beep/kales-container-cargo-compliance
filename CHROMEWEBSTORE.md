## Detailed Description
Container & Flatbed Semi-Trailer Compliance Checker (by KALES) lets freight forwarders, dry van dispatchers, and overseas trailer buyers verify whether container, pallet, and cargo loads comply with local highway weight, height, length, CSC/VGM, and axle regulations.

Key Features:
1. 5 Container & Cargo Semi-Trailer Load Profiles: Switch between 20ft/40ft/40HQ/45HQ Skeletal Container Chassis, Flatbed Semi-Trailers, Drop-Sidewall Trailers, High-Stake Fence Trailers, and Dry Van / Enclosed Box Trailers.
2. Dry Van Volumetric CBM, ISO 668 CSC/VGM & Pallet Floor Planner: Dedicated cube-first mode for lightweight voluminous Dry Van freight (custom cargo density t/m³, usable internal CBM, cube utilization %), top-down Euro (1200×800 mm) and ISO/US (1200×1000 mm) pallet layouts, ISO 668 / CSC MGW & SOLAS VGM checks, EN 12642 XL / EN 12195-1 cargo securing notes, and ISO 1726 / ECE R58 design boundaries.
3. 28 Regional Road Law, Bridge Formula & Container Height Compliance Checks: Verify steer, drive, kingpin, trailer axle-group, GCW/GVW, length, width, and 40HQ/45HQ container height exemptions across China (GB 1589-2016), EU (96/53/EC 44 t intermodal), UK (C&U 44 t), US (23 CFR 658.17), Japan (44 t container / 4.1 m route exemptions), Australia (NHVR HVNL), Taiwan (Art. 38/79), Vietnam (Circular 39/2024 4.35 m container), Indonesia (PP 55/2012), Philippines (RA 8794), Thailand (DOH 50.5 t), Mexico (NOM-012), Brazil (CONTRAN 882/2021), Colombia (Res. 4100/2004), Peru (D.S. 058-2003-MTC), Chile (D.S. 158 MOP), Tanzania (EAC/TANROADS), East Africa (EAC), Southern Africa (SADC), South Africa (NRTR Reg. 240), Nigeria, Guinea, Ghana, Kazakhstan (EAEU), Russia (PP No. 2200), UAE (138/2023), and Saudi Arabia (RGA).
4. Suspension & ETRTO Tyre Rating Verification: Compare air and mechanical leaf-spring axles against 12R22.5, 11.00R20, 12.00R20, 315/80R22.5, 385/65R22.5 super-single, and 235/75R17.5 low-CG tyres.
